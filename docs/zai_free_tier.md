# ZAI Free Tier — Research Summary

**Last verified**: August 2026.

## What is ZAI?

ZAI (Zhipu AI) provides LLM inference, particularly for GLM models.

**Base URL**: `https://open.bigmodel.cn/api/paas/v4`

## Free Tier Limits

| Limit | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Free tokens | Limited | Varies by model |

## Available Models

- GLM-4 series
- CogVideo
- And more

## Getting an API Key

1. Sign up at [open.bigmodel.cn](https://open.bigmodel.cn)
2. Export: `export ZAI_API_KEY="..."`

## Known Gotchas

- Free tier is limited
- Pricing varies by model
- Primarily focused on Chinese language models
